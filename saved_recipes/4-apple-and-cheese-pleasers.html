Apple and Cheese Pleasers

  • Print
  • Email

Servings: 24 servings



Ingredients:-

  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2/3 cup butter or margarine,softened
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cup quick cooking oats
  • 4 oz cheddar, shredded; 1 c
  • 3/4 cup raisins
  • 1 cup chopped apple

Method:-

    • Combine flour, butter, sugar, egg, vanilla, cinnamon, baking powder and salt in large bowl; mix well.
    • Add oats, cheese and raisins; mix well.
    • Stir in apple.
    • Drop by heaping tablespoons onto ungreased cookie sheet
    • Bake in preheated moderate oven (375degrees) 15 minutes or until golden brown.
    • Store in tightly covered container in refrigerator or in loosely covered container at room temperature.

    Note:-
